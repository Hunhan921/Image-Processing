WorldQuant University (WQU) is a private, non-profit, online university offering free data science education, notably a Master of Science in Financial Engineering (MFE), leveraging technology to provide global access to talent in finance and data fields, with its programs accredited in the U.S. and serving as a pipeline for the quantitative investment firm WorldQuant. Its core mission is to democratize opportunities, making skills accessible regardless of location, under the motto "Talent is equally distributed globally, but opportunity is not"

In this Applied AI Lab, I will learn the core concepts behind neural networks in computer vision applications through 6 hands-on projects with Pytorch and Transformers:

- Project 1 is about Wildlife Conservation in Côte d'Ivoire. I’ll assess a data science competition designed to help scientists track animals in a wildlife preserve.
- Project 2 takes me to Uganda, where I’ll build a neural network to classify images of crop disease.
- From there, I travel to Bangladesh for Project 3 to create an object detection model to monitor traffic in Dhaka.
- In Project 4, I’ll perform face detection and recognition tasks using a video interview of Indian Olympic boxer Mary Kom.
- Project 5 takes me to Spain, where I’ll use neural networks to generate a variety of medical images.
- For Project 6, I’ll explore social media marketing in the United States.

Projects will include supervised learning tasks, such as image classification and object detection, and unsupervised learning tasks, such as generative AI for image synthesis. I will apply self-regulated learning techniques for exploring software libraries and debugging code. Throughout these project work, I will also consider the ethical and environmental impact of computer vision models, and learn techniques to build models emphasizing fairness and sustainability. Finally, I’ll learn from practicing data scientists about the central values and concerns in the field.

Once I have completed the Lab, I hope I'll be equipped with end-to-end computer vision skills, from data preparation and ETL processes to model development and deployment, enabling me to solve real-world challenges across industries.
